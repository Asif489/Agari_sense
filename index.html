ABOUT AGRISENSE

Agriculture needs better information.

Agricultural decisions are influenced by multiple interacting factors. AgriSense is designed to bring these factors together into a single decision-support workflow rather than relying on one isolated measurement.

01

Multiple Data Sources

Soil, weather, environmental and agricultural information can be combined to create a broader view of field conditions.

02

AI-Based Analysis

Machine learning models can transform the collected information into predictions, rankings and decision-oriented insights.

03

Understandable Decisions

The platform is designed to present not only a recommendation but also the factors that contribute to the decision.

PLATFORM

From agricultural data to useful decisions.

AgriSense is structured as a modular decision-support system. Individual models can be trained and connected to the platform through the backend.
